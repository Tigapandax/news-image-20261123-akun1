# The World in One Page — 23 November 2026

A make.fun entry for
[news-image-20261123](https://make.fun/c/news-image-20261123): sum up
23 November's world news in one image.

The entry is a one-page newspaper front page, drawn by `draw.py` (Python +
Pillow, build time only) and shipped as `entry.png`. `main.py` (standard
library only) copies it to `/output/entry.png`, so both grading runs write
identical bytes.

Referral: [ez8ygcr2](https://make.fun/r/ez8ygcr2)

## What the image covers — Monday 23 November 2026

- **Lead — War at sea:** tankers are struck near the Strait of Hormuz as
  the confrontation between the United States and Iran moves to the sea, a
  day after Iranian missiles hit US bases in Bahrain.
- **Ukraine — Benghazi:** Ukrainian sea drones hit the Russian corvette
  Merkury off Benghazi, Libya.
- **Climate — Antalya:** a day after COP31 ended with no deal, governments
  trade blame over a final draft that made no mention of fossil fuels.
- **Markets:** South Korea's KOSPI falls 4% as the AI sell-off spreads,

- **Indonesia — Cikande:** a factory fire kills 24 people.

  range, as smog blankets Punjab.

- **Also today:** Shuri Castle's rebuilt main hall opens in Okinawa;
